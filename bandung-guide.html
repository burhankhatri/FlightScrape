WalkableHidden gem

Selasar Sunaryo Art Space & Kopi Selasar

Walk · ~5 min Rp30k

Bandung's best contemporary-art space, terraced into the Dago hills by ITB artist Sunaryo — rotating shows, an open-air amphitheatre and a sculpture garden. The attached Kopi Selasar is a serene wood-and-greenery coffee garden, and the whole thing is basically at the end of your road.

GalleriesKopi SelasarSculpture garden
◷ Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00 (closed Mon)

TipThe one real attraction you can walk to. Cafe is free to enter without the gallery ticket; order the namesake Kopi Selasar.

In TahuraHidden gem

Warung Kopi Armor — coffee in the pines

Walk · ~10 min Rp57k

A tiny wooden coffee shack set right inside the pine forest of Tahura Djuanda — outdoor tables under towering trees, cool mountain air and cheap single-origin Indonesian coffee. The closest proper nature-cafe to your place, and the perfect warm-up before the caves or Tebing Keraton.

Forest coffeeCool airPre-hike stop
◷ Weekday mornings, misty & quiet

TipThe entry fee is the Tahura gate (foreigners ~Rp57k) — so make a half-day of it and walk to the caves or the cliff after.

ForestClassic

Tahura Ir. H. Djuanda forest park

Grab ~Rp25k · 10 min Rp57k

A 590-hectare ribbon of jungle threading from Dago Pakar all the way to Maribaya, with one shaded forest spine linking caves, waterfalls and the Cikapundung river right at the city's edge. One ticket buys a whole cool, green day of walking — the single best piece of nature within reach of your bed.

Dago Pakar gateRiver trailPicnic flats
◷ Weekday 07:00–10:00

TipEnter via the Dago Pakar gate for the flattest start. Rent a bamboo walking stick at the entrance; download the map since signal dies inside.

CavesLocal favourite

Goa Jepang & Goa Belanda — the war caves

Grab ~Rp28k · 12 min Free

Two hand-dug tunnel complexes a short walk apart inside Tahura: the Dutch Cave, a 144-metre grid of pitch-black hydro-plant tunnels from 1901, and the rawer, eerier Japanese Cave, bored by forced labour in 1942 as a military bunker. Rent a flashlight guy at the mouth and the dark does the rest.

Goa BelandaGoa JepangFlashlight guide
◷ Midday — a cool escape from the sun

TipBoth sit ~15 min walk from the Dago Pakar gate. Hire one senter (flashlight) guide for ~Rp5–10k to cover both rather than fumbling with your phone torch.

SunriseLocal favourite

Tebing Keraton — the cliff

Grab ~Rp30k · 12 min Rp17k

A jutting rock ledge at 1,200 m on the edge of the Tahura pine forest, staring straight across the green Maribaya valley toward the Lembang Fault. Come before dawn and the whole forest below drowns in a sea of fog that burns gold as the sun climbs the far ridge — it's the postcard of North Bandung, and it's in your own village.

SunriseSea of fogCliff platform
◷ Sunrise 05:30 — arrive by 05:00

TipGrab cars can't reach the gate; the last 1.5 km finishes on an ojek (~Rp20–30k) or on foot. Go on a weekday to beat the railed-viewpoint queue.

HiddenHidden gem

Curug Dago — the secret waterfall

Grab ~Rp35k · 18 min Rp11k

A secluded 12-metre fall on the Cikapundung, famous for two Thai-script stone inscriptions left when Kings Rama V and Rama VII visited over a century ago. Recently tidied up, it's a quiet, history-soaked pocket of jungle far less mobbed than the rest of Tahura.

WaterfallThai inscriptionsQuiet jungle
◷ Weekday mornings

TipAccess is from the lower Curug Dago entrance off Jl. Dago Pojok, not the main Pakar gate. Ask for the 'prasasti Thailand' and the warden points you to the stones.

City viewLocal favourite

Dago Pakar view-cafés — the city-lights cluster

Grab ~Rp28k · 10 min Free

A knot of hillside cafés a few minutes from you — Sierra, Congo, The Stone Cafe, The Valley — all perched for the same trick: the whole Bandung basin spread below, turning into a glittering carpet of lights after dark. Sierra has the widest panorama; Congo and Stone sit side by side with live music; The Valley does romantic dinners.

SierraCongoThe Stone CafeThe Valley
◷ Arrive ~17:00 for sunset → city lights

TipRequest the lowest/front terrace for an unobstructed view, and bring a layer — it gets genuinely cold after dark up here. On a budget? De Grote's 3rd-floor rooftop has the same view for less.

City lightsLocal favourite

Bukit Moko & Puncak Bintang

Grab ~Rp45k · 28 min Rp15k

The highest accessible hilltop on Bandung's east rim (~1,442 m), where a short pine-lined walk leads to the star-shaped Puncak Bintang platform. After dark the entire basin spreads out as a shimmering carpet of light below your feet; just below sits Warung Daweung, arguably the highest warung in the city, where you sip hot bandrek over a cliff-edge rail.

Puncak BintangWarung DaweungPine forest
◷ Sunset into blue hour, or sunrise above the fog

TipThe final road is steep and narrow — confirm your Grab driver will actually go all the way up before booking, and arrange the ride back as cars thin out after dark.

24 hoursLocal favourite

Caringin Tilu (Cartil)

Grab ~Rp40k · 22 min Free

A free-to-enter hillside strip in Padasuka Atas lined with open-air warungs and cafés, all aimed straight at the Bandung basin. It's the local go-to for cheap food and an unobstructed front-row seat to the city-light bowl — open round the clock, so a 2 a.m. coffee over the lights is entirely on the table.

City lightsCheap warungsOpen 24h
◷ Late afternoon into night

TipNo entry ticket — you only pay for what you order. Claim an edge table at Dapur Caringin Tilu before dusk and nurse a coffee as the lights switch on.

Sunrise eatsLocal favourite

Punclut — misty hilltop breakfast

Grab ~Rp42k · 22 min Free

A breezy ridge above Ciumbuleuit packed with saung-style warungs serving the signature nasi merah 'binggol' — red rice in a bamboo basket with sambal and lalapan — eaten cross-legged on hilltop platforms with the valley on one side and the city on the other. Newer cafés like Tagme add a TikTok-famous sunset view next door.

Nasi merahMisty sunriseTagme café
◷ Misty breakfast 07:00, or sunset

TipCome for an early breakfast when the ridge is wrapped in cloud and far quieter than the sunset rush. Expect a small local parking levy (~Rp5–15k) on Punclut roads.

Hidden hikeGatekept

Sesar Lembang ridge trail

Walk · ~15 min Rp15k

A genuinely underrated ridgeline walk tracing the active Lembang Fault scarp, linking Tebing Keraton, Bukit Moko and Batu Lonceng through shady pine forest with 360° valley views. Hiking clubs love it for sunrise above the mist with almost none of the theme-park crowds — and you can start it near Tebing Keraton, partly on foot from home.

Fault-line ridgeSunrise hikeNo crowds
◷ Pre-dawn start; dry days only

TipNo single ticket gate — small fees at the Tebing Keraton / Puncak Bintang access points. The clay trail gets slippery, so go on a dry day and bring water; no Grab along the ridge itself.

Into the forest

The Tahura traverse to Maribaya

2 spots
Signature hikeLocal favourite

The Dago → Maribaya forest walk (Curug Omas)

Grab ~Rp45k · 25 min Rp17k

The classic Tahura traverse: ~5.5 km of cool forest path from the Dago Pakar gate to Curug Omas, where the Cikawari and Ciwagun rivers crash together and plunge past iron footbridges strung over the gorge. The roar, spray and mossy cliffs make it the natural turnaround of the best day-hike in Bandung.

Curug OmasForest pathIron bridges
◷ Weekday mornings, dry season for footing

TipWalk it one-way (2–2.5 hrs) from Dago Pakar and Grab back from the Maribaya side — much better than doubling back. Not up for the full hike? Grab straight to the Maribaya end; the falls are a short walk down.

Maribaya Hot Springs & The Lodge

Grab ~Rp65k · 35 min Rp45k

A forested river gorge where small waterfalls tumble through ferns and you soak tired feet in natural hot pools fed by Tangkuban Perahu's geothermal heat. Up the ridge, The Lodge Maribaya adds pine-clad walking trails and viral photo rigs — a 'sky tree' bench and zip-bike that dangle you out over the valley.

Hot poolsThe LodgeValley swings

Active craterClassic

Tangkuban Perahu — the volcano

Grab ~Rp130k · 70 min Rp200k

An active stratovolcano whose 'upturned boat' silhouette gives it its name, where you walk right up to the railing of Kawah Ratu and stare down into a 500-m bowl of hissing sulphur steam and boiling mud. Vendors cook eggs on the warm earth, and you can scramble down to Kawah Domas to dip a hand in a bubbling spring.

Kawah RatuKawah DomasSulphur steam
◷ Arrive 07:00–08:00 before the crater clouds over

TipForeigner tickets run far above local prices (~Rp200k weekday). Grab is very scarce at the top gate — have your driver wait or pre-book the return, and bring a mask for the sulphur.

Orchid Forest Cikole

Grab ~Rp95k · 55 min Rp100k

A genuine highland pine forest threaded with the 'Forest Walk' suspension bridges, 20,000+ orchids, a rabbit forest and a wood-and-glass garden that glows with fairy lights at dusk. Tucked inside is Golden Pine, a European-style glasshouse café where you drink coffee among towering pines and mountain mist.

Suspension bridgesOrchidsGolden Pine café
◷ 9:00 opening on a weekday; stay for dusk lights

A beautifully landscaped valley of bamboo architecture, terraced flower gardens and a small lake where you paddle wooden boats beneath the hills. Birdcage-shaped dining pods hang over the greenery, and quiet paths climb to cooler pine-and-bamboo viewpoints away from the crowds.

TurquoiseHidden gem

Sanghyang Heuleut, Kenit & Tikoro

Grab ~Rp320k · 110 min Rp20k

A string of milky-turquoise plunge pools cupped between giant boulders in the Saguling gorge — rumoured in Sundanese myth to be where angels bathed — deep enough to leap into off the rocks. Downstream, Sanghyang Kenit runs emerald-clear into a cave mouth (the safe swim, with tubing and life jackets), while Tikoro is the eerie 'throat' where the Citarum vanishes underground.

Heuleut poolsKenit tubingTikoro cave
◷ Dry-season mornings 8–11 for the brightest jade

TipFrom the PLTA Saguling car park it's a rocky 30–45 min trek. There is NO return ride at the trailhead — have your driver wait or charter a car for the day. Grippy shoes; skip it after heavy rain when the water browns and currents rise.

TallestLocal favourite

Curug Cimahi — the Rainbow Waterfall

Grab ~Rp150k · 70 min Rp20k

At 87 m this is the tallest waterfall in greater Bandung, thundering down a fern-draped cliff you reach by descending ~580 forest steps past chattering monkeys. Around 1 p.m. the spray throws a real rainbow; after 6 p.m. coloured spotlights wash the falls, which is how it earned its 'Pelangi' nickname.

87 m dropRainbow mistNight colours
◷ ~14:00 for a daytime rainbow, or after 18:00 for lights

TipThe easiest big waterfall to reach — Grab can usually drop and collect at the Curug Pelangi gate. The climb back UP the stairs is the only real work; pace it.

AdventureHidden gem

Curug Tilu Leuwi Opat & Curug Aseupan

Grab ~Rp140k · 65 min Rp20k

'Three waterfalls, four pools' — tea-coloured leuwi and small caves linked by suspension bridges and river crossings in the Parongpong hills, with body-rafting and trekking through genuinely wild, dripping greenery. Nearby and quieter still is Curug Aseupan, a slender cone-shaped fall you reach by wading a shin-deep river and scrambling a steep ladder.

3 falls, 4 poolsBody-raftingCurug Aseupan
◷ Dry-season mornings — safer crossings

TipReached via Ciwangun Indah Camp, Parongpong. Grab gets you to the entrance but pickups are unreliable up here — ask your driver to wait or save a local ojek number. Wear shoes you can soak.

CampHidden gem

Curug Layung — pine-forest camp falls

Grab ~Rp150k · 75 min Rp10k

A gentle, low-pressure waterfall in a cool pine forest at 1,400 m on the flank of Mt. Burangrang — less a big-drama cascade than a mellow, almost alpine hideaway of pine-scented air, open meadow and a shallow stream. Beloved as a riverside camping spot above Cisarua.

Pine meadowRiverside campSunset

Mini-CappadociaLocal favourite

Citatah karst — Stone Garden, Gua Pawon & Tebing Hawu

Grab ~Rp230k · 95 min Rp15k

A surreal field of wind-carved limestone pinnacles atop Gunung Masigit — the eroded remnant of a 20-million-year-old coral reef, often likened to a mini-Cappadocia, with the whole Padalarang valley dropping away below. Just downhill, Gua Pawon hid ~9,500-year-old human skeletons, and the dramatic Tebing Hawu rock arch draws Bandung's hardcore climbers.

Stone GardenGua Pawon caveTebing Hawu arch
◷ Sunrise or golden hour for soft light on the rock

TipGrab can drop you at the Stone Garden car park, but return pickups are unreliable — keep your driver and do all three as one karst loop. Wear real shoes; the rock is sharp.

Epic missionGatekept

Curug Malela — the 'Little Niagara'

Grab ~Rp600k · 210 min Rp10k

A genuinely jaw-dropping ~50-m-tall, ~70-m-wide curtain of water fanning across black rock in the remote far west of the regency, near the Cianjur border. Its sheer scale and the brutal journey to reach it make standing at its base feel like a hard-won secret — Bandung's true 'Little Niagara'.

50 m curtainFar westHard-won
◷ Dry season; start at dawn — it's a full day

TipThe hardest reach here. Grab can't serve it: charter a car to Gununghalu, then the last broken stretch is best by ojek (~Rp75k round trip). Budget ~4 hrs each way — not a casual day trip.

Living here for a month

The practical stuff for a long stay up in the Dago Atas hills.

  • Your trip lands in peak dry season: late July through August brings sunny ~28°C days, but Dago Atas sits high (~1,100 m) so nights drop to 14–18°C — August is the heart of the local 'bediding' cold snap. Pack a jacket, a sweater and long pants for evenings, plus sunscreen for the strong highland UV.
  • Install both Grab and Gojek. Grab handles foreign cards better; Gojek often has more drivers. Up in Ciburial's steep lanes, motorbike (GoRide/GrabBike) is far easier to flag than a car — especially after dark — so book return rides early and save a trusted driver's WhatsApp.
  • Rough fares: short city car rides ~Rp20–40k, longer cross-city or hill runs ~Rp50–100k, motorbike hops ~Rp10–20k. Car drivers thin out late at night in the hills.
  • Connectivity: Telkomsel has the best signal up in the hills. A 30-day ~20–50 GB tourist SIM or eSIM runs ~USD 17–35 and needs your passport to register; an eSIM bought before arrival is easiest.
  • Money: Bandung is heavily QRIS-cashless but foreign cards can't scan the QR — keep cash for warungs, markets, parking and small rides. Prefer BCA ATMs (Mandiri adds a ~Rp50k foreign-card fee); per-withdrawal limit ~Rp2.5–3M. Budget roughly Rp16,500 per USD.
  • Health & safety: Bandung is generally safe — the real daily risk is traffic, so cross deliberately and always wear the ojek helmet. Tap water isn't drinkable; dengue is present, so use repellent. RS Hasan Sadikin and RS Borromeus are the main hospitals — carry travel insurance.
  • Culture: West Java is devout and Muslim-majority. Most food is halal by default; pork is clearly labelled at the few non-halal spots; alcohol is available but discreet. Expect the call to prayer five times a day, including a pre-dawn one (~04:30) carrying across the valley — earplugs if you sleep light.
  • Doorstep notes: nearest Alfamart/Indomaret minimarkets are down toward Dago/Dago Pakar, so stock up on a trip down. Tahura Djuanda forest is right there — just don't feed the monkeys; they snatch food and bags. Ramadan 2026 ended in March, so it won't affect a July–August stay.
