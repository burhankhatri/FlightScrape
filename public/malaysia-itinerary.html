Notes & tips

  • Group savings — 4-way splits help most on (a) vans & private cars, (b) Mantanani / Pulau Kapas day-boat charter, (c) Danum Valley packages, (d) lodging. Food is per-person but sharing dishes at hawker stalls trims another 10–15%.
  • Monsoon caveat — Terengganu's east coast is best Mar–Oct. July is fine; Nov–Feb is washed-out.
  • Live-price warning — Domestic flight prices above are valid as of fetch time. Hit flighthelper.vercel.app before booking; the Book buttons deep-link to Google Flights.
  • Booking order — Lock the Danum Valley lodge first (sells out 2+ months ahead, harder for groups). Domestic flights easy on AirAsia/Batik 1 month out. Hotels last.
  • Pack — Reef-safe sunscreen, dry bag, head-torch (Danum + jungle waterfalls), light long-sleeves, MYR cash for east-coast warungs & boat operators (no card).
  • Skipped from your list — Maliau Basin needs 3-night minimum & permits; Lankayan / Tenggol / Pangkor Laut Resort are exclusive (different budget tier); Bohey Dulang & Sibu/Tinggi need a dedicated 3–4 day block.
